What changed in this version

• Compose the expanded player only while opening or visible, preserve its saved UI state, and stop screen subscriptions while the UI is in the background.
• Warm only the next one or two local tracks, evict obsolete preload sources, and honor shuffle order without copying the whole queue.
• Defer automatic analysis during playback and clear disposable artwork caches on running-memory pressure.
• Optional AI connections: Google AI Studio, Groq, Cerebras, Hugging Face, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude and xAI Grok. Choose connections, priority, fallback and request rotation.
• Review exactly what metadata will be sent, then ask for playlists, songs, albums, genres, library edits or sharing suggestions. AI controls appear on home only after enabling a connection.
• Review library edits individually, preserve durable undo records, and use the system trash confirmation on supported Android 11+ files. AI cannot execute commands or modify audio files.
